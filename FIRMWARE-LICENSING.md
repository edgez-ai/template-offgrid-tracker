# Firmware ownership and licenses

Device source, pinned platform dependencies, licensing checks and build artifacts
are maintained in [device-firmware](https://github.com/edgez-ai/device-firmware). See its
[firmware licensing notice](https://github.com/edgez-ai/device-firmware/blob/main/FIRMWARE-LICENSING.md).

The app's Apache-2.0 license does not relicense third-party device firmware,
radio libraries, SDKs or board-configuration blobs.
